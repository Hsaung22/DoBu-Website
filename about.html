WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN A GYM

DoBu Martial Arts is a professional martial arts and fitness centre designed for people of different ages and experience levels.

Our goal is to provide a safe, friendly and motivating environment where members can improve physical fitness, martial arts skills, confidence and discipline.

Our training programme includes Jiu-jitsu, Judo, Karate and Muay Thai, supported by experienced instructors and modern facilities.
